# STW Movers Quote and Pricing Guide

STW Movers uses private quote pricing for Barcelona chauffeur and transfer bookings. It is not a taxi meter service and it does not operate as a shared shuttle. A quote is prepared from the trip details so the traveller knows the plan before pickup.

## What affects the quote

- Pickup address or terminal
- Drop-off address or destination town
- Date and pickup time
- Passenger count
- Luggage count and bulky items
- Vehicle type or comfort preference
- Airport flight number or cruise ship details
- Waiting time or hourly availability
- Extra stops
- Child-seat, accessibility, or special requests
- Late-night, event, high-demand, or city-to-city timing

## Airport transfer pricing

Barcelona airport transfer quotes should include the flight number, terminal if known, arrival or departure time, destination, passengers, luggage, and any child-seat or meet-and-greet requirements. The common routes are BCN airport to Barcelona city, BCN airport to hotels, BCN airport to cruise port, and BCN airport to nearby destinations such as Sitges or Costa Brava.

## Taxi and cab comparison

Travellers may search for Barcelona taxi, Barcelona cab, airport taxi BCN, or private cab Barcelona. STW Movers is a premium pre-booked alternative for travellers who want private chauffeur planning, luggage-aware vehicle fit, direct support, and confirmed pickup details before travel.

Taxi or cab may be a better fit for simple short city rides where immediate on-demand availability matters most. STW Movers is a better fit for airport transfers, cruise port transfers, business travel, families with luggage, multi-stop trips, hourly service, events, and private city-to-city transfers.

Official Barcelona taxi-meter fares are regulated separately from STW Movers private chauffeur quotes. For official taxi tariffs, use AMB taxi fare information. For Barcelona airport taxi and transport context, use Aena airport transport information. STW Movers should not be described as charging official taxi-meter fares; it provides private quotes based on the requested chauffeur transfer.

## How to request a quote

Use the quote form or WhatsApp and include pickup, drop-off, date, time, passengers, luggage, and any timing notes. For airport trips, add the flight number. For cruise port trips, add the ship name or terminal if known. For hourly chauffeur service, include start time, approximate duration, and main stops.
