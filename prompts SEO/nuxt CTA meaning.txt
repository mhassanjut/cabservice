CTA means Call to Action.

In SEO/marketing context, it refers to a prompt designed to encourage users to take a specific action—usually by clicking a button or link.

In your example:

"Do you want a tour?" → Links to bcncitytour.com

That CTA is intended to guide visitors toward booking or exploring tours.

Common CTA Examples
Book Now
Get a Quote
Contact Us
Start Free Trial
Learn More
Do You Want a Tour?

In navbar/header SEO strategy, CTAs are often placed prominently because they improve:

Conversion rate (more clicks/leads)
User navigation
Internal/cross-domain traffic flow
