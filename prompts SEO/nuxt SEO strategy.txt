Project Overview

The business operates two related websites targeting the Barcelona transportation and tourism market:

stwmovers.com – Primary platform for cab/transfer booking services
bcncitytour.com – Primary platform for Barcelona city tours and tourism services
Service Scope
Cab / Transfer Services (stwmovers.com)

The cab booking platform will support:

Airport Transfers
Barcelona Airport → Barcelona city
Barcelona Airport → Other cities in Spain
City Transfers
Within Barcelona city
Barcelona → Other cities
General Cab Booking
On-demand transportation within service regions
Tour Services (bcncitytour.com)

The tourism platform will support:

Barcelona city tours
Guided/local tourism packages
Tour booking and itinerary information
Frontend / SEO Strategy
Phase 1: Static SEO-Focused Website Launch

Initially, both platforms will launch as frontend-only static websites with:

SEO-optimized landing pages
Static informational/service content
Conversion-oriented CTAs for future booking workflows

A strategic internal cross-promotion model will be used between both domains:

On stwmovers.com
Homepage/top navbar will include:
"Do you want a tour?" → Links to bcncitytour.com
Static homepage content will:
Focus primarily on cab/transfer keywords
Subtly mention tourism/tour availability
On bcncitytour.com

"Want to book a cab?" → Links to stwmovers.com
Static homepage content will:
Focus primarily on tourism/tour keywords

SEO Objective

The goal is to:

Target both tourism and transportation keyword clusters
Build authority for each domain in its primary niche
Use contextual cross-linking between domains to:
Capture overlapping search intent
Funnel users between complementary services
Strengthen overall topical relevance across both brands
Long-Term Vision

After SEO traction is established:

Introduce dynamic booking functionality/backend systems
