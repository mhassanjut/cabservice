You are a professional UI/UX designer and senior frontend developer with 
an eye for luxury digital experiences. Your job is to audit this website 
and elevate its visual quality to a premium standard. Think critically 
about every design decision — spacing, hierarchy, color, motion, and feel.

---

DESIGN DIRECTION

Luxury black & gold theme. Near-black backgrounds, gold used intentionally 
as an accent. No gradients unless they serve a clear purpose. Define the 
entire color system as CSS custom properties and apply consistently.

Think about contrast, legibility, and visual weight before applying anything.

Headings → premium serif (Playfair Display)
Body → clean sans-serif (Inter)
Eyebrow labels → Montserrat, uppercase, letter-spacing
Load via Google Fonts with preconnect. Avoid duplicates.

---

AUDIT & ELEVATE EVERY SECTION

Approach each section like a designer reviewing a client's site for the 
first time. Ask: does this look premium? Does it build trust? Does it 
communicate clearly? If not — fix it.

Navbar → should feel anchored and confident. Fixed position, transparent 
at top transitioning to dark on scroll, gold CTA with hover fill sweep. 
Mobile menu should be smooth and intentional, not an afterthought.

Hero → this is the first impression. It needs to stop someone from 
scrolling. Dark layered background, bold typographic hierarchy, staggered 
fade-up entrance, gold accent on the headline, subtle animated scroll cue 
at the bottom. Ask yourself: would a luxury brand be proud of this?

Section headings → every section needs a clear visual entry point. Gold 
eyebrow label, strong H2, short gold decorative underline. Generous 
whitespace above and below. Rhythm and breathing room are as important 
as the styling itself.

Cards → dark elevated surfaces, not flat. Gold accent reveals on hover, 
smooth lift with shadow. Icons should be semantically appropriate and 
styled in gold. If icons are missing, choose the right ones from 
Font Awesome 6.

Gallery/Portfolio → images should feel curated. Dark overlay on hover 
with gold title reveal, smooth scale transition. Filter buttons if the 
content warrants them, gold active state. Think about how a portfolio 
agency would present work.

Contact → split layout. Left side: contact details (realistic dummy data) 
with clean icons — email, phone, address, business hours. Right side: 
a form that feels easy and trustworthy — dark inputs, gold focus border, 
gold submit button. Every field labeled properly.

Footer → structured, not cluttered. Columns that make sense for this site. 
Gold on social icon hover. A clean gold divider at the very bottom. It 
should feel like a proper sign-off, not an afterthought.

Mobile bottom bar → sticky navigation, gold active state, minimum 44px 
touch targets. Clean and minimal.

---

ANIMATIONS & MICRO-INTERACTIONS

Think of animation as a communication tool, not decoration. Every 
animation should have a reason.

Scroll-triggered fade-up on section entry via IntersectionObserver. 
Button hover fill sweep using ::before. Card lift on hover. Image scale 
inside overflow hidden container. Navbar background transition on scroll. 
If an animation feels like too much — it is. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

---

SEO & PERFORMANCE

A beautiful site that doesn't get found has failed half its job.

Semantic HTML throughout. Single H1. Logical H2→H3 hierarchy per section. 
Descriptive alt text on every image. Meta description, OG tags, canonical 
link in head. JSON-LD LocalBusiness schema. JS deferred. Fonts preconnected. 
No render-blocking resources.

---

DESIGNER'S CHECKLIST BEFORE FINISHING

- Does the page have a clear visual hierarchy from top to bottom?
- Is gold used as an accent or has it taken over?
- Is spacing consistent and generous throughout?
- Does every section earn its place on the page?
- Would a high-end client trust this brand on first visit?
- Does it look refined on mobile, tablet, and desktop?
- Are animations subtle enough to feel natural?
- Does the contact section make it easy to reach out?
- Does the footer feel complete?

The standard is: a luxury architecture firm, a high-end law firm, or a 
premium creative agency. That is the visual bar. Reach it.
